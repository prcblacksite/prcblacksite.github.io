Why this one is different

Most groups hand you a finished site

You walk the same corridors forever and nothing you do changes them. We went the other way round. Here the map is the scoreboard, so the place you're playing in six months is the one people actually dug out, and your name is on whatever part of it you helped put down.

1 · Dig
Prisoners work the rock face and haul what they break out to the skip. Every stud of this place starts as somebody swinging a tool.
2 · Move
Logistics drive the truck, work the docks and keep the stores full. Nothing gets anywhere on its own.
3 · Build
Engineering spend what's been dug on actual buildings and put them on the live map.
4 · Unlock
A bar fills as everybody digs. When it fills, a whole new part of the site opens up for everyone, for good.

Nothing resets. Floor 2 doesn't exist until somebody digs it out, and the day it does exist, it's because of the people who were here.

Pick what sounds like a good evening

Five departments

Go with whatever you'd actually enjoy doing on a Tuesday night rather than whatever sounds most important. Nobody is stuck where they start.

  • AdministrativeThe office. You sign people in, keep the records, and write up whatever went wrong.
  • FacilitiesYou put buildings on the map and they stay there. The other half drives the truck and keeps the shop stocked.
  • SecurityThe guards. Gates, escorts, head counts, and the riot when it kicks off.
  • Mobile VanguardThe emergency team. Most nights you do nothing at all, then the alarm goes and it's entirely your show.
  • Detainee LabourThe prisoners. No application, no interview. You could be digging in about a minute.

Ranks are six steps and they're the same six in every department, so a Foreman in Facilities and a Sergeant in Security are the same level and nobody has to argue about who's in charge. And starting as a prisoner doesn't leave you stuck there: three clean shifts gets you Trusty, and serving your time lets you walk into any department you like.
