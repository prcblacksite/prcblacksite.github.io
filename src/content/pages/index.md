## Most groups hand you a finished site

You walk the same corridors forever and nothing you do changes them. We went the other way round. Here the map is the scoreboard, so the place you play in six months is the one people actually dug out, and your name is on whatever part of it you helped put down.

{{ loop }}

> **Nothing resets.** Floor 2 doesn't exist until somebody digs it out, and the day it does exist, it's because of the people who were here.

{{ status-board }}

## Five departments

Go with whatever you'd enjoy on a Tuesday night rather than whatever sounds most important. Nobody is stuck where they start.

{{ department-cards }}

Ranks are six steps, the same six in every department, so a Foreman in Facilities and a Sergeant in Security are the same level. Starting as a prisoner doesn't leave you stuck either: three clean shifts makes you a Trusty, and serving your time lets you walk into any department you like.
