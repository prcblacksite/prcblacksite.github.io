Everyone starts here. The camp is the tutorial, and the only thing to learn is the dig.

You step off the boat, you're processed in about a minute, and you're holding a tool. Detainee Labour is a real department with a real ladder, and a detainee can get somewhere without stopping being a detainee.

## The detainee ladder

{{ camp-ladder }}

In the fiction, a release is a sentence discharged and a contract converted: the Corporation offers site employment to discharged detainees because the licence makes leaving the island difficult.

## Who runs it

A small staff administers the camp: the **Superintendent of Labour** sets the work programme and chairs release boards, the **Labour Overseer** assigns gangs to faces and signs off Trusties, and the **Muster Clerk** takes the roll and logs output. Security guards it. They are not the same people.

## Consequences inside the fiction

The camp needs consequences to feel like a camp. Every one of them is short and administrative.

{{ consequences }}

Solitary is capped at five minutes, for everyone, with no exceptions. A punishment that takes a player out of the game for longer than that isn't roleplay.
