## After you are accepted

You start at G0, on trial, and your department's channel opens. Someone says hello in it within a day. Your first step up is a short procedure check and one supervised shift. See [Moving up](/wiki/promotion/).

You also ask to join your department's Roblox community. The site accepts you and sets your rank there, so the game knows who you are.

## If you're not sure

Say what you'd rather be doing on an average evening in `#faq-desk`, and somebody will match you to a department. That works far better than reading the list again.
