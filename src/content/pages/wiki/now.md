The Discord is open. The game opens on **Saturday 31 October 2026**. This page is the current state of things and the first one to change.

## What you can do today

- **Join the server** and link your Roblox account. The camp channel opens the moment you accept the rules.
- **Apply to a department** with `/apply` once you're verified. You hear back within 24 hours.
- **Run something.** Director seats are open, and they go to people who were here early.
- **Come to a playtest.** Until the doors, every Wednesday and Saturday op is a playtest.

## Before the doors

{{ playtests }}

Ops are at 19:00 UTC. In the server, `/schedule` shows every one of these in your own time zone.

## What exists on day one

A supply pier, an intake hut, the camp, the checkpoint called the Wire, the Cut, and one corridor into the mountain. Everything else gets dug out and built by the people playing, and once it's built it stays.

## Founder

Everyone who joins before the doors keeps **Founder** for good. It stops being given out the day the doors open and is never granted again. It sits beside the ranks, not on them: a Founder can be a detainee or a director and keeps it either way.

## Not yet

- **Research and Containment** is a real department and it is locked. It opens when the dig reaches something worth researching.
- **Floor 2 and below** don't exist until they are dug.
- **Freeform building, a second truck, trains and steerable boats** are not in the launch version.

What comes after, and what each thing waits on, is on [the roadmap](/roadmap/).
