Six steps, the same six in every department, so a Foreman and a Sergeant are the same level and nobody argues about who is in charge.

Every member holds two things: **what they do**, which is their department, and **how far they have got**, which is their grade. The grade carries the authority. G3 means "runs a shift and the people on it" everywhere.

## Command

Above the ladder are three Command seats: **Overseer** (C1), **Continuum Director** (C2) and **Observer** (C3). They are appointments, not promotions, and no promotion reaches them.
